Hello, I'm Quinten.

I investigate how light can be used to program chemical reactions with high precision, enabling new materials and manufacturing technologies.

Featured research

ChemRxiv · 2026 · Preprint

Antagonistic Dual-Wavelength Tomographic Volumetric Additive Manufacturing

Quinten Thijssen, Antonio J. Ortega, Felix Wechsler, et al.

This work introduces antagonistic dual-wavelength control to tomographic volumetric printing. One wavelength drives polymerization while a second locally suppresses it, providing an additional route for controlling material formation inside the resin volume.

Nature Communications · 2026

Volumetric 3D Printing of a Fluoropolymer and Closed-Loop Chemical Recycling of its Fluorinated Content

Quinten Thijssen, Antonio Jaen-Ortega, Nele Pien, et al.

This study combines volumetric fabrication of a fluorinated polymer with closed-loop recovery of its fluorinated content. It connects rapid three-dimensional manufacturing with a chemical route for recovering a high-value molecular component.

Chemical Reviews · 2026

Light-Based 3D Printing of Polyesters: From Synthesis to Fabrication

Quinten Thijssen, Astrid Quaak, Bart Bijleveld, et al.

This review examines the chemistry underlying light-based 3D printing of polyesters, from polymer synthesis and photochemical functionalization to resin formulation and fabrication.
